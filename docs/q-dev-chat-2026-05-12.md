The issue is the ampersand (`&`) — it's a reserved character in SSML/XML. You need to escape it as `&amp;`.

Try this:

```xml
<speak>Welcome to today's One Year Bible reading for May 12, 2026. Today's passages are Judges 20:36-Ruth 1 &amp; Acts 5. Let's begin.</speak>
```

Alternatively, you could just replace `&` with the word "and":

```xml
<speak>Welcome to today's One Year Bible reading for May 12, 2026. Today's passages are Judges 20:36 through Ruth 1 and Acts 5. Let's begin.</speak>
```

The second version actually sounds more natural when spoken aloud anyway. When we build the batch script, we'll make sure to handle this automatically — either escaping special characters or replacing `&` with "and" in all 365 scripts.
